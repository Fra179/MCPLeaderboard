## Course Overview

**Multicore Programming** focuses on the technical foundations of parallel programming, a necessity driven by the physical limits of modern hardware scaling. As single-thread performance gains have plateaued, performance improvements now rely on multicore and distributed architectures rather than frequency scaling.
